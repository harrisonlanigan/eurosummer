lay of the land

small things worth knowing

Italy

  • Many shops shut Sun + Mon morning. Riposo 1—4pm in smaller towns.
  • Coperto on every bill — small cover charge, normal.
  • Tipping not expected. Round up if you want.
  • Rome has free public fountains (nasoni) — drinkable, fill your bottle.
  • Lunch ~1pm, dinner from 7:30pm. Aperitivo 6—8pm.

Spain

  • Dinner is LATE — 9pm earliest, 10pm normal. Lunch is the big meal.
  • Tapas: order a few, share, move on to the next bar.
  • Card almost everywhere; cash for tiny stalls + Barcelona city tax.
  • Sunday most shops closed except in tourist drags.

France

  • Lots closed Sun + sometimes Mon. Bakeries close one day a week (varies).
  • Tipping not expected, service compris is included.
  • Say bonjour when entering shops — actually matters.
  • Paris in late June: long daylight, sunset ~9:55pm. Plan late.

Montenegro

  • Uses EUR (not in EU but adopted it).
  • Cash useful — family konobas often card-shy.
  • Bay roads tight + winding. Add buffer to travel times.
  • Fortress hike: go at sunrise or after 5pm. Midday is brutal.
